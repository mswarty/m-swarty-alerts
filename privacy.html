M Swarty Alerts Privacy Policy

M Swarty Alerts is a private personal notification program used to send recurring personal safety and system-status SMS alerts to the program owner and to a small number of family members who have agreed to receive them. This Privacy Policy describes what information the program handles and how it is used.

Information collected

Recipient phone numbers are collected only for the purpose of sending M Swarty Alerts SMS messages. Phone numbers are obtained directly from each recipient (verbally or in writing) and are added manually by the program owner. M Swarty Alerts does not use any web form, online sign-up, or public opt-in mechanism to collect phone numbers.

Basic message delivery logs may be stored, including message timestamp, recipient phone number, and delivery status. These logs are used only to verify that alerts were delivered and to troubleshoot delivery issues.

How information is used

Recipient phone numbers are used only to send personal safety and system-status alerts, such as home safety alerts, coastal flooding alerts, high tide alerts, automation status alerts, and automation failure notices. Phone numbers are not used for marketing, promotional, or sales-related messages of any kind.

Information sharing

Recipient phone numbers and message data are not sold, rented, or shared with any third party for marketing or promotional purposes.

Information may be shared with service providers (such as the SMS delivery provider used to transmit messages) only as necessary to deliver M Swarty Alerts SMS messages. These service providers may process phone numbers and message content solely to carry out SMS delivery on behalf of the program, and not for any independent marketing or promotional use.

No mobile information, phone numbers, or consent data is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Opt-out

Recipients can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any M Swarty Alerts SMS message. After replying STOP, the recipient's phone number will no longer receive M Swarty Alerts messages.

Help

Recipients can reply HELP to any M Swarty Alerts SMS message to receive help information, or contact the program owner directly.
