M Swarty Alerts Privacy Policy

M Swarty Alerts is a private personal notification system used to send operational and safety alerts to myself and family members who have agreed to receive them.

Information collected

We store recipient phone numbers only for the purpose of sending SMS alerts. We may also store basic message delivery logs, such as message time, recipient phone number, and delivery status.

How information is used

Phone numbers are used only to send personal operational and safety alerts, including coastal flooding alerts, high tide alerts, script run status alerts, stock screener completion alerts, and system failure alerts.

Information sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share recipient phone numbers or message data with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be shared with service providers only as necessary to deliver SMS messages.

Opt-out

Recipients may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any SMS message.

Contact

For questions or support, recipients may reply HELP to any SMS message or contact the sender directly.
