Program name

M Swarty Alerts

Program description

M Swarty Alerts is a private personal notification system used to send operational and safety alerts to myself and family members who have agreed to receive them. Alerts may include coastal flooding risk, high tide warnings, script run status, stock screener completion status, and system failure notifications.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on alert conditions and automation activity. Messages may be occasional or recurring.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-out instructions

Reply STOP to opt out of receiving future SMS messages.

Help instructions

Reply HELP for help or additional information.

Consent

Recipients are added only after providing direct verbal or written permission to receive these personal alerts.

Privacy

Recipient phone numbers are used only for sending these alerts and are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.
