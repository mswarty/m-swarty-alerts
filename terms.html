M Swarty Alerts Terms and Conditions

Program name

M Swarty Alerts

Program description

M Swarty Alerts is a private notification program that sends recurring personal safety and system-status SMS alerts to the program owner and to a small number of family members who have agreed to receive them. Alerts may include home safety alerts, coastal flooding risk, high tide warnings, automation status alerts, and automation failure notices.

Messages sent through M Swarty Alerts are not marketing, not promotional, and not sales-related. The program is used solely for personal safety and system-status notifications.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. Messages are sent only when alert conditions or automation events occur, and the number of messages per recipient is not fixed.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply.

Help instructions

Reply HELP for help or additional information about M Swarty Alerts.

Opt-out instructions

Reply STOP at any time to opt out of receiving future M Swarty Alerts SMS messages. After replying STOP, no further messages will be sent to that phone number.

Consent

M Swarty Alerts does not use any web form, online sign-up, or public opt-in mechanism. Consent is obtained directly from each recipient, verbally or in writing, before that recipient's phone number is manually added to the program by the program owner. No phone number is added without prior consent from the recipient. See the Opt-In Instructions page for the exact invitation text and step-by-step opt-in process used for each recipient.

Privacy

Recipient phone numbers are used only to deliver M Swarty Alerts SMS messages. Recipient phone numbers and message data are not sold, rented, or shared with any third party for marketing or promotional purposes. See the Privacy Policy for additional detail.
