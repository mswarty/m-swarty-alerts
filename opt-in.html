M Swarty Alerts Opt-In Instructions

M Swarty Alerts is a private recurring SMS alert program for personal safety and system-status notifications. Recipients are personally known to the program owner and are added only after directly agreeing to receive messages.

This page documents how each recipient opts in to receive M Swarty Alerts SMS messages. It is provided as public evidence of the program's Call to Action (CTA) and consent process.

Program name

M Swarty Alerts

How recipients opt in

  1. The program owner contacts the recipient directly by conversation, text message, or email.
  2. Before the recipient is added, the recipient is shown or sent the opt-in invitation text below.
  3. The recipient must directly reply or confirm that they agree to receive M Swarty Alerts SMS messages.
  4. The program owner then manually adds the recipient phone number to the private alert recipient list.
  5. No recipient is added without prior consent.

Sample invitation / Call to Action text

Each recipient is shown or sent the following invitation message before being added to the program:

M Swarty Alerts Invitation:
You are invited to receive recurring M Swarty Alerts SMS messages for personal safety and system-status notifications, including coastal flooding risk, high tide alerts, home safety alerts, automation status alerts, and automation failure notices. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to opt out. Please reply YES if you agree to receive these messages.

Opt-in confirmation message

Once a recipient has agreed and has been added to the program, the following confirmation message is sent to that recipient:

M Swarty Alerts: You are opted in to recurring personal safety and system-status alerts. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to opt out.

Additional information

  • M Swarty Alerts does not use a public web form.
  • M Swarty Alerts does not use marketing or promotional messages.
  • Consent is voluntary and not required for any purchase, account, or service.
  • Recipient phone numbers are not sold, rented, or shared for marketing or promotional purposes.
